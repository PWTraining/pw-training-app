# Parking Lot

Ideas, scope creep, and "later" items that surfaced mid-build. Nothing here gets built until it's pulled out deliberately — see Operating Rule 4.

- **Meal Plan Template workbook (Trinity system)** — Paul's `🔱 Meal Plan - Template.xlsx` contains a full FoodDB, meal plan builder, functional ranges/bloods tracker, KPI charts, and a business tracker sheet, well beyond the nutrition-audit scope in the master brief. Worth a dedicated look in Phase 6 (macros calculator / business health) to see what's reusable, but not before.
- **Client-selectable testing metrics catalogue** — Paul wants clients to add metrics like bench press 1RM, 5km time, deadlift 1RM, sit-and-reach themselves. Needs a small admin-managed metric catalogue (name, unit, direction of improvement) rather than a hardcoded list. Scope for Phase 5 (testing data tab), not Phase 0/1.
- **GoDaddy DNS records for app.pwworld.com.au / send.pwworld.com.au** — not added yet, deferred until Vercel + Resend accounts exist.
- **Weather widget, vision/workbooks section, offers page, resources hub, macros calculator, business health dashboard** — all explicitly Phase 6 per the master brief's own build order. Noted here only so nobody starts them early.
